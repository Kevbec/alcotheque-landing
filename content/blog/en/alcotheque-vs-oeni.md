# Alcotheque vs Oeni: Two Wine Apps, Two Very Different Goals

Oeni has built a genuinely impressive product. Two million downloads, a 4.7 star rating from 30,000 reviews, 3D cellar visualization, food and wine pairing recommendations across 5,000 dishes, real-time market valuation, and a sommelier ambassador who has won best sommelier of Italy twice. If you collect wine and want a beautifully designed tool to manage it, Oeni is one of the most complete options available.

Alcotheque is built around a different question. Not just wine, but wine and spirits together. And not just inventory, but the full lifecycle of a bottle, including where it came from, who gave it, and what it is worth.

This comparison is honest. Both apps are well made. The right choice depends entirely on what is actually on your shelves.

---

## What Oeni Is Built For

Oeni positions itself as the number one wine cellar management app, and by download count it may well be. The product is centered on three core experiences.

The first is inventory management. A database of 500,000 bottles lets most collectors find their wines quickly. You add the bottle, specify the vintage, log the purchase price, and Oeni begins tracking.

The second is timing. Oeni analyzes the aging phase of each bottle and recommends when to open it at its peak. This is powered by proprietary algorithms developed over more than a year of research, and it is one of the features that most clearly differentiates Oeni from simpler inventory tools.

The third is pairing. With over 5,000 food and wine pairings, Oeni acts as a personal sommelier, recommending which bottles from your cellar best match a specific dish or occasion. This is a genuinely useful feature for collectors who entertain regularly.

Oeni also has 3D cellar visualization, real-time market valuation, Excel import, and is available on iOS, Android, and web. It is a well-rounded and mature product.

What Oeni does not do: spirits. The platform is built for wine and remains wine-focused. If you have bottles of whisky, rum, gin, or cognac alongside your wine, Oeni will not help you manage them.

---

## What Alcotheque Is Built For

Alcotheque was built for a collector who does not separate wine from spirits. The inventory is unified, one interface for everything on your shelves, whether that is a Burgundy grand cru, a Hampden rum, or a bottle of Hendrick's gin.

The AI label scanning is the core differentiator on the input side. Point your iPhone camera at any bottle label, wine or spirits, and the app extracts the name, type, vintage, producer, and other details automatically. This works on bottles that are not in any pre-built database, including older expressions, independent bottlers, and regional producers.

Storage location tracking assigns bottles to named locations rather than building a 3D replica of your cellar. Your cave, your armoire, your holiday home. Simple and fast to set up, with no need to recreate your physical space digitally.

The gift tracking feature is dedicated and detailed. You log every bottle received with the giver's name, the occasion, and the date. You also log bottles you have offered as gifts. Over time this becomes one of the most personal and useful parts of your collection record.

Privacy is a foundational design choice. No public profile, no community layer, no data shared with third parties. Your collection belongs entirely to you.

Alcotheque is currently available on iOS, with Android on the roadmap. It is available in 8 languages, covering the major European markets.

## On the Wine Experience: Where Oeni Goes Deeper

For pure wine collectors, Oeni offers a depth that Alcotheque does not match on the wine-specific side.

The aging phase feature is genuinely useful. Knowing when to open a bottle at its peak requires data and algorithms that take time to build. Oeni has invested heavily in this and it shows. If you have a serious wine cellar and you regularly make decisions about when to open versus when to hold, this feature alone justifies exploring Oeni.

The food pairing engine is similarly impressive. 5,000 pairings covering a huge range of dishes, analyzed against your actual collection to find the best match for tonight's dinner. This is the sommelier experience made practical and accessible.

The 3D visualization is the most visually striking feature and the most useful for collectors with significant physical infrastructure. If you have a dedicated cellar with multiple racks, being able to see your collection in three dimensions and locate any bottle with a tap solves a real problem.

---

## On Spirits: The Clearest Difference

If your collection includes whisky, rum, gin, cognac, or tequila alongside your wine, Oeni is simply not the right tool. There is no spirits support and no indication this will change in the near future.

Alcotheque manages wine and spirits with equal depth. The same AI scanning, location tracking, gift logging, and collection analytics apply to a bottle of Foursquare rum as to a Château Margaux. If your shelf is mixed, Alcotheque is the only unified option.

---

## On Pricing: A Note on Transparency

Oeni's pricing is not publicly listed on their website. You can start a 30-day free trial of the premium features, but the cost after that is not disclosed before you sign up. This is a legitimate business choice, but it does make direct comparison difficult.

Alcotheque's pricing is transparent. The free plan covers 15 bottles. The premium plan costs less than €1 per month and unlocks unlimited bottles, locations, and AI scans. There is no trial period because there is no need for one at that price.

## Who Should Use Oeni

If wine is your primary focus and you want the most complete wine cellar management experience available, Oeni is an excellent choice. The aging phase recommendations, food pairing engine, and 3D visualization are all features that no competitor currently matches in one product.

It is also the right choice if you need Android support right now, or if you want web access to your collection from any browser.

## Who Should Use Alcotheque

If your collection includes spirits alongside wine, Alcotheque is the only serious option. There is no other app that manages a mixed wine and spirits collection with the same level of detail and care.

If you collect wine but also regularly give or receive bottles as gifts, the [dedicated gift tracking feature](https://www.alcotheque.app/en/blog/how-to-track-wine-gifts) is something Oeni does not replicate.

If you value transparent pricing and want to know exactly what you are paying before you commit, Alcotheque's pricing model is straightforward and accessible.

And if AI label scanning matters to you, particularly for bottles that may not be in a pre-built database, Alcotheque's OCR approach works on any bottle regardless of whether it has been catalogued.

## Can You Use Both?

Yes. Some collectors use Oeni for its wine-specific depth, particularly the aging recommendations and food pairings, and Alcotheque for their spirits collection and gift tracking. The two apps address different aspects of the same collection and do not conflict.

If you are starting fresh with a mixed collection, Alcotheque is the simpler starting point. One app, one interface, everything in one place.

## FAQ

### Does Oeni support spirits like whisky or rum?
No. Oeni is focused exclusively on wine. If your collection includes spirits, Alcotheque is the only app that manages both wine and spirits in one unified interface.
