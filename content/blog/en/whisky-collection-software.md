# Best Whisky Collection Software and Apps in 2026

Managing a whisky collection used to mean a notebook or a spreadsheet. Today there are dedicated apps, barcode scanners, AI label readers, and cloud-synced inventories that make the whole process significantly easier — if you pick the right tool.

This guide compares the main options available in 2026: the humble spreadsheet, OnlyDrams, CellarTracker, and Alcotheque. Each one suits a different kind of collector. Understanding which fits your situation will save you time and frustration.

---

## Why Your Collection Needs More Than a Spreadsheet

Most whisky collectors start with a spreadsheet. It is free, flexible, and familiar. For the first twenty or thirty bottles, it works fine.

The problems start when your collection grows or becomes more complex. A spreadsheet has no concept of physical storage locations — it cannot tell you that the bottle you are looking for is in the second shelf of the armoire in the spare room, not in the main cave. It cannot scan a label. It cannot automatically estimate what your collection is worth today. And it certainly cannot track which bottles were gifts, from whom, and for what occasion.

The move from spreadsheet to dedicated software is not about features for their own sake. It is about having an inventory that actually reflects how you use your collection — finding bottles quickly, tracking what you spend and what things are worth, and building a record that means something over time.

## The Main Options in 2026

### OnlyDrams

OnlyDrams is a mobile app (iOS and Android) built specifically for spirits collectors. Its core strength is a database of over 56,000 bottles with verified pricing data and a barcode scanner that pulls up detailed bottle information instantly. The Bottle Drops feature — direct partnerships with distilleries for release announcements — is genuinely useful for collectors chasing allocated releases. The Infinity Bottle tracker, which documents custom blends pour by pour, is unique in the market.

**Best for:** collectors who focus exclusively on spirits, particularly whisky and bourbon, and want a large verified database with market pricing.

**Limitations:** no wine support, no AI label scanning (barcode only), English-language only, community-focused which is not everyone's preference.

**Pricing:** free plan with unlimited bottles and database access. Elite plan at $4.99 per month unlocks custom fields, enhanced pricing, and exports.

### CellarTracker

CellarTracker is the oldest and most established platform in the wine world, founded in 2003 with over 13 million community reviews. For wine collectors it is unmatched in community depth and data richness. For whisky collectors, it is the wrong tool. The platform is built for wine, its community is wine-focused, and using it for spirits means working around a product that was never designed for your category. Worth mentioning because many serious collectors know it — but not recommended for whisky.

### Alcotheque

Alcotheque is an iOS app built for collectors who manage both wine and spirits — or spirits only — in one unified interface. The key differentiator is AI label scanning: point your iPhone camera at any bottle label, wine or spirits, and the app extracts the name, type, vintage, producer, and other details automatically. This works on bottles that are not in any pre-built database, including older expressions, independent bottlers, and craft distilleries with limited distribution.

Storage location tracking assigns bottles to named locations — your cave, your armoire, your holiday home — so you can find any bottle instantly. Gift tracking logs which bottles you received and offered, with context on who gave them and when. Collection valuation tracks purchase price and estimated current value across your entire inventory.

**Best for:** collectors who manage wine and spirits together, collectors with rare or unusual bottles that may not be in barcode databases, European collectors who want the app in French, German, Dutch, Spanish, Italian, or Portuguese, and collectors who want a private inventory with no social layer.

**Limitations:** iOS only for now (Android on the roadmap), no 56,000-bottle spirits database, no barcode scanning.

**Pricing:** free plan for 15 bottles. Premium plan under €1 per month, unlimited bottles and locations.

## How to Choose

**Choose OnlyDrams if:**
- You collect spirits exclusively, particularly whisky and bourbon
- You want a large verified database with secondary market pricing
- You collect primarily mainstream releases from known distilleries
- You want Android support right now

**Choose CellarTracker if:**
- Wine is your primary focus and you want the deepest community data available
- Spirits are not part of your collection

**Choose Alcotheque if:**
- Your collection includes both wine and spirits
- You collect rare, older, or independent bottler expressions that may not be in barcode databases
- You want AI scanning that works on any bottle without a pre-built database
- You are a European collector who wants the app in your language
- You want a private inventory with no community or social dimension
- Price is a factor — at under €1 per month, it is significantly more affordable than the alternatives

**Stick with a spreadsheet if:**
- You have fewer than 20 bottles and no plans to grow
- You want complete control over your data format

## A Note on Independent Bottlers

One area where the software choice matters more than most collectors realize: independent bottlers. Gordon & MacPhail, Signatory, Cadenhead's, Velier, and dozens of smaller operations release expressions that often do not appear in commercial databases. If you collect these — and serious whisky collectors often do — a barcode scanner will frequently return no result.

AI label scanning handles this differently. Because it reads the label directly rather than looking up a database entry, it can extract useful information from virtually any bottle, even one that has never been catalogued commercially. For collectors who venture beyond the mainstream releases, this matters.

## FAQ

### What is the best software to manage a whisky collection?
It depends on your collection. If you collect spirits only and want a large database with market prices, OnlyDrams is a strong choice. If your collection includes wine alongside spirits, or you want AI label scanning that works on rare bottles, Alcotheque is the only app that covers both in one interface.

### Can I use a spreadsheet to manage my whisky collection?
Yes, but spreadsheets become limiting quickly. They have no concept of physical storage locations, no label scanning, no automatic valuation, and no way to track gifts. Most collectors outgrow a spreadsheet around 30 to 50 bottles.

### Does CellarTracker work for whisky?
CellarTracker is built for wine. It has a large community and drink window data but does not officially support spirits. Using it for whisky means working around a tool designed for a different category.

### Is there a free app to track a whisky collection?
Yes. Both Alcotheque and OnlyDrams have free plans. Alcotheque's free plan covers 15 bottles. OnlyDrams' free plan includes unlimited bottles with database access and barcode scanning.

### What whisky collection app works on iPhone in 2026?
Alcotheque and OnlyDrams are both available on iPhone. Alcotheque is iOS-only for now with Android on the roadmap. OnlyDrams is available on both iOS and Android.
